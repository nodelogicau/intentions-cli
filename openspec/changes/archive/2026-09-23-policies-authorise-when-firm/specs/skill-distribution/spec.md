## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The skill states that a policy authorises nothing until firm
The embedded skill SHALL state, where it describes firming and selecting under a policy, that a policy the harness drafts is tentative and authorises nothing until the person firms it by their own act, that the refusal names the draft and the command the person runs, and that setting a firm policy tentative suspends it while retiring it ends it, either withdrawing what rested on it.

#### Scenario: Skill states the policy rule
- **WHEN** the embedded skill is read
- **THEN** its rules on firming and on selecting under a policy say a drafted policy authorises nothing until the person firms it, and name suspend and end
